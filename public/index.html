Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

  • Player accounts: your name, email address, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text) when you sign up on My Profile.
  • Team/league admin logins: a team code or admin username, and an email/password for league admins.
  • Interest / join-a-league forms: name, contact number, email, and your Playtomic level, if you fill one in.
  • Photos: a profile photo if you add one, and your team's kit design photos if a captain uploads them.
  • Match data: line-ups, scores, and results entered by captains and admins as part of running the league.
  • Push notifications: if you turn these on, your device's browser registers a notification address with us — this isn't linked to your name or email.

Why we collect it

To run the league you're part of: showing fixtures and results, letting you log in and see your own record, sending you match reminders you've opted into, and letting an admin contact you about your team. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for anything beyond running the leagues on this site.

Who else sees it

  • Payments: if a captain sends you a payment link, you enter your card/bank details directly on PayFast's own page — we never see or store them.
  • Email: account and password-reset emails are sent through our email provider.
  • Sign-in with Google/Facebook: only if you choose that option, to confirm your name and email.
  • Hosting: your data is stored with our hosting and database providers, who may process it outside South Africa.

Cookies and analytics

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in — the site can't work without it, and it's not optional. With your separate consent (the banner you saw when you first visited), we also use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the app is used. Neither loads until you accept, and you can change your mind any time from the link at the bottom of this page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can delete your own account (login, email, and photo) yourself from My Profile at any time — match results and team rosters you were part of stay as part of the league's shared record, the same way they would if you'd simply stopped playing, rather than being erased for everyone else who played in those matches. For anything else, contact us at teampadelsocial@gmail.com.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact teampadelsocial@gmail.com.

Every league, including ones already hidden or incognito. Hidden is for data-only imports (still counts for ratings, but never shows in search, login, or anyone's "Your leagues").
