Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Team Padel ("we", "us") runs teampadelsports.com and its installed app version, used to organise and play in padel leagues. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account details — if you sign up for a player account: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Sign in with Google or Facebook — if you choose to sign in this way, we receive only your name and your confirmed email address from that provider (and an identifier that lets us recognise you next time). We never receive your Google or Facebook password, and we don't access your contacts, files, posts or any other data from them. You can use an email and password instead at any time.
  • League and match data — team rosters, line-ups, scores, results, and season history for the leagues you play in or manage.
  • Team login codes — a captain's access code for a team, used to sign in without a full account.
  • Push notification subscriptions — if you opt in, your device's push subscription (an address and keys your browser generates) so we can send you real notifications. This is entirely optional and can be turned off at any time from the same place you turned it on.
  • Payments — where a league charges registration fees, payments are processed directly by PayFast. We don't see or store your card details — PayFast handles that under its own privacy policy.
  • Usage analytics — we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages visited, general device/browser type). This is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.
  • Cookies — a session cookie keeps you signed in. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the leagues you're part of — line-ups, scores, standings, and results.
  • To send you notifications (email or push) about your team's matches and results. If you captain a team, these are emailed to your account address; you can turn that off in My Profile → Notifications.
  • To process registration payments, where applicable, via PayFast.
  • To understand and improve how the site is used, via aggregated analytics.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

Who we share it with

Only the third parties needed to run the service:

  • Upstash — our database host, where league and account data is stored.
  • PayFast — processes registration payments where a league charges a fee.
  • Google Analytics — aggregated usage analytics.
  • Other players and captains in your own league can see your name, team, and match results, the same way they would on a printed scoresheet — that's the normal, intended use of the app.

Your choices

  • You can turn push notifications on or off at any time from the Notifications tab or My Profile.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time — contact us below.
  • You can decline or clear cookies in your browser, though staying signed in relies on the session cookie working.

Data retention

We keep league and match data for as long as a league remains active or historical records are meaningful (e.g. season history, career stats). Account data is kept until you ask us to remove it.

Children's privacy

Team Padel is intended for general use by padel players and league organisers, not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
